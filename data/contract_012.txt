Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between BayPoint Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Aurelia Health Services, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of May 24, 2026.
1. Services: Vendor shall provide health services and related administrative support as described in Exhibit A, consistent with applicable standards of care.
2. Lines of Business Covered: The services shall apply to the Health Plan's Medicare Advantage and Medicaid lines of business.
3. Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue through May 23, 2028.
4. Renewal: Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the end of the then-current term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
5. Fees and Payment: Health Plan shall pay Vendor in accordance with the fee schedule in Exhibit B; all payments are Net 60 from receipt of correct invoice.
6. Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing 120 days' written notice to the other party.
7. Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
8. Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of Proprietary Information and Protected Health Information in accordance with law and this Agreement.
9. Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify and hold harmless Health Plan from claims arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement; Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's negligence.
10. Insurance: Vendor shall maintain professional liability, general liability, and cyber liability insurance with limits reasonably acceptable to Health Plan and shall provide certificates upon request.
11. Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's relevant records, books, and systems, including claims and billing data, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours.
12. Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws, regulations, and Health Plan policies, including those governing Medicare Advantage and Medicaid programs.
13. Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain technical and organizational measures to protect electronic protected health information in accordance with applicable law.
14. Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent; Vendor remains responsible for subcontractor performance.
15. Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification and willful misconduct, neither party's liability shall exceed the fees paid under this Agreement in the preceding twelve months.
16. Notices: All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at the addresses set forth herein by hand, certified mail, or overnight courier.
17. Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to an affiliate or successor in interest.
18. Severability: If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
19. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, including exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to its subject matter and supersedes prior agreements.
20. Amendment: This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.
21. Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve disputes; if unresolved, disputes shall be resolved in the state and federal courts located in the State of Ohio.
22. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
