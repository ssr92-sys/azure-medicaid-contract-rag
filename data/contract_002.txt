Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into by and between Northbridge Health Plan ("Health Plan") and Medora Solutions, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of May 18, 2026.
Scope of Services: Vendor will provide services to the Health Plan for the Medicaid line of business as described in Exhibit A.
Term: The Initial Term of this Agreement shall commence on May 18, 2026 and shall end on May 17, 2027.
Auto-Renewal: Unless either party provides written notice of nonrenewal at least 30 days prior to the end of the then-current term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
Compensation: The Health Plan shall pay Vendor in accordance with the fees set forth in Exhibit B.
Payment Terms: All undisputed invoices shall be payable Net 30 from the invoice date.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 30 days' prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary and member information and shall not disclose such information except as required by law or with prior written consent.
Data Protection: Vendor shall comply with applicable state and federal privacy and security requirements and with Health Plan policies regarding protected health information.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Health Plan from and against all claims, liabilities, losses and expenses arising out of Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Health Plan Indemnity: The Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's negligence or breach of this Agreement.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, and cyber liability insurance in limits customary for the services and shall provide certificates upon request.
Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's records relevant to this Agreement upon reasonable notice during normal business hours.
Subcontracting: Vendor may subcontract services only with Health Plan's prior written consent and remains fully responsible for subcontractor performance.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable laws, rules, and regulations, including those governing Medicaid.
Remedies: Remedies set forth herein are cumulative and in addition to any other rights or remedies available at law or in equity.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations and gross negligence or willful misconduct, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid in the preceding twelve months.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in Exhibit C and shall be effective upon receipt when sent by certified mail, courier, or email with confirmation.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Severability: If any provision is held invalid, the remainder of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, including exhibits, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes prior agreements.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York without regard to conflict of law principles.
