Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Northbridge Health Plan ("Health Plan") and Suncoast Care Services, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of February 16, 2026.
Scope: Vendor shall provide services to members of the Health Plan limited to the Medicaid line of business in accordance with the terms herein.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and shall continue through February 15, 2029.
Renewal: This Agreement will automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the then-current term's expiration.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving 120 days' prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within 30 days of written notice, or immediately for insolvency or fraud.
Payment: The Health Plan shall pay Vendor pursuant to the rates and schedule attached as Exhibit A, and all undisputed invoices shall be paid Net 30 from receipt of a proper invoice.
Taxes: Each party is responsible for its own taxes, and Vendor shall invoice applicable taxes separately where required by law.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information and Protected Health Information in compliance with applicable law, and shall not disclose such information except as required by law or with prior written consent.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Health Plan and its affiliates from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, losses, costs and expenses arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement; the Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for its negligent acts or breaches.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, and cyber liability insurance in amounts customary for the services provided and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit Rights: The Health Plan, or its designee, shall have the right to audit Vendor's records, books, and systems related to the services and charges under this Agreement during normal business hours upon reasonable notice, and Vendor shall cooperate and provide access.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all federal and state laws, rules and regulations applicable to Medicaid services, including privacy, security and program integrity requirements.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material obligations without the Health Plan's prior written consent, and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractors.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect electronic protected health information and report any security incidents promptly to the Health Plan.
Remedies: Remedies available to the parties shall be cumulative, and the prevailing party in any dispute shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnity, breach of confidentiality, willful misconduct or gross negligence, neither party's liability shall exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve months preceding the claim.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered by certified mail, courier, or email to the parties' designated representatives as set out in Exhibit B, and shall be effective upon receipt.
Assignment: Neither party shall assign this Agreement without the other's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and not an employee, agent or partner of the Health Plan, and Vendor shall be responsible for its personnel.
Records Retention: Vendor shall retain records related to the services and claims for a minimum of seven years or longer if required by law and shall provide copies upon reasonable request.
Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to conflict of law principles.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with Exhibits A and B, constitutes the entire agreement between Northbridge Health Plan and Suncoast Care Services, LLC and supersedes all prior understandings.
