VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Hudson Valley Care Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC ("Vendor").
Effective date: January 29, 2026.
The Initial Term shall commence on the Effective Date and end on January 28, 2027.
The parties acknowledge that this Agreement does NOT automatically renew after the Initial Term.
Scope of Services: Vendor will provide clinical and administrative services to support the Health Plan's Medicare Advantage and Medicaid lines of business.
Compensation: Health Plan will pay Vendor pursuant to the fee schedules attached and according to Payment terms: Net 45.
Vendor shall perform services in accordance with applicable federal and state requirements, including Medicare Advantage and Medicaid program rules.
Termination: Either party may terminate for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of Protected Health Information and confidential business information and shall use the information only for purposes of this Agreement.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards consistent with HIPAA and any applicable State of New York requirements.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan from third party claims arising from Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement, and Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for Health Plan's negligence.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability and cyber liability insurance in commercially reasonable amounts and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit Rights: Health Plan or its designees shall have the right to audit Vendor's books, records and systems relevant to the services and charges during the term and for a reasonable period thereafter.
Records Retention: Vendor shall retain all records necessary to verify compliance with this Agreement and applicable law for the period required by law or a minimum of seven years.
Compliance and Licensing: Vendor represents it is duly licensed and will comply with all applicable laws, rules and regulations in the State of New York and federal law.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract any material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent; Vendor remains responsible for subcontractor performance.
Limitation of Liability: Except for willful misconduct or indemnity obligations, neither party's liability shall exceed the total fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve months preceding the claim.
Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at the addresses set forth in the signature block, by hand, certified mail, or overnight courier.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve disputes promptly by escalation to authorized representatives before pursuing formal remedies.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York without regard to conflicts of law principles.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to an affiliate or in connection with a sale of substantially all assets.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with the exhibits and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement and supersedes all prior agreements between the parties.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
