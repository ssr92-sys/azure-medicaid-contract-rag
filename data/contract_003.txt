VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement is entered into by and between Evergreen Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and SunCrest Health Solutions, LLC ("Vendor").
Effective date: July 15, 2026
Initial term ends: July 14, 2029
Lines of business covered: Commercial
Payment terms: Net 60
Termination for convenience notice: 90 days
Governing law: State of Ohio
1. Services: Vendor shall provide the services set forth in applicable Statements of Work and shall perform in a professional, timely and workmanlike manner.
2. Term and Renewal: The initial term shall run from July 15, 2026 through July 14, 2029 and, unless terminated as provided herein, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
3. Compensation: Invoices shall be submitted in accordance with applicable Statement(s) of Work and paid Net 60; disputes must be raised promptly and undisputed portions shall be paid when due.
4. Termination: Either party may terminate for material breach upon thirty days' written notice if the breach is not cured, and either party may terminate for convenience by providing ninety days' written notice.
5. Confidentiality: Each party shall protect confidential information of the other and shall not disclose it except to its employees, agents or subcontractors who need to know and are bound to protect such information.
6. Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan, its directors, officers and employees against claims arising from Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
7. Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance, including general liability, professional liability, cyber liability and workers' compensation, and shall furnish certificates evidencing such coverage upon request.
8. Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right to inspect and audit Vendor's relevant books, records and systems during normal business hours upon reasonable notice to verify performance and charges.
9. Data Protection and Compliance: Vendor shall implement appropriate safeguards for protected health information and comply with all applicable federal and state privacy and security laws.
10. Warranties: Vendor represents that it has the authority to enter this Agreement and that the services will conform to applicable standards and specifications in the Statements of Work.
11. Limitation of Liability: Except for liability arising from indemnification obligations, confidentiality breaches, or willful misconduct, neither party shall be liable for consequential or punitive damages.
12. Subcontracting and Assignment: Vendor may engage subcontractors with Health Plan's prior written consent and shall remain responsible for subcontractor performance and compliance with this Agreement.
13. Notices: Notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in the applicable Statement of Work or as otherwise designated, and shall be effective upon receipt.
14. Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith, and if unresolved, may pursue remedies in the courts located within the State of Ohio.
15. Severability and Survival: If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in effect, and obligations regarding confidentiality, indemnification, payment and audit rights shall survive termination.
16. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with all Statements of Work and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between Evergreen Health Plan, Inc. and SunCrest Health Solutions, LLC with respect to the services.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective date.
