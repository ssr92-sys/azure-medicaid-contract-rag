Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Clearwater Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and SunCrest Health Solutions, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of March 23, 2025.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on March 22, 2026.
Non-renewal: This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Scope: Vendor will provide services to the Health Plan for the Commercial line of business as set forth in any Statement of Work.
Compensation: Fees for services shall be invoiced by Vendor and paid by the Health Plan in accordance with Net 45 payment terms, with payment due within forty-five days of receipt of a properly submitted invoice.
Termination for convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for cause: Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice of breach.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's confidential information and protected health information and shall not disclose such information except as required by law.
Data security and privacy: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards reasonably designed to protect electronic protected health information and shall comply with applicable privacy and security laws and regulations.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan and its officers, directors and employees from and against any third-party claims, liabilities, losses, damages and expenses arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.
Limitation of liability: Except as to indemnification for third-party claims and obligations that cannot be limited by law, neither party shall be liable for consequential, incidental or punitive damages.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain and provide certificates evidencing commercial general liability, professional liability/errors and omissions, and cyber/privacy insurance in commercially reasonable amounts, and upon request shall furnish proof of such insurance to Health Plan.
Audit rights: Health Plan shall have the right, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours, to audit Vendor's books, records and systems relating to performance and fees under this Agreement, subject to confidentiality protections.
Compliance: Vendor represents and warrants that it will comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations, including those applicable to healthcare operations, in the performance of services.
Independent contractor: The parties are independent contractors and nothing in this Agreement creates a partnership, joint venture or employer-employee relationship.
Subcontracting: Vendor shall not subcontract material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent and shall remain responsible for performance of any approved subcontractor.
Records retention: Vendor shall retain records relating to services and billing for a minimum of six years following the date of issuance of the final invoice or as otherwise required by law.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, provided that Health Plan may assign to an affiliate or successor in interest.
Notices: All notices required or permitted hereunder shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, return receipt requested, or overnight courier to the addresses set forth in the applicable purchase order or to the parties' business addresses and shall be effective upon receipt.
Severability: If any provision is held invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
Entire agreement: This Agreement, together with any appendices and Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings and communications.
Amendment: Any amendment to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
Governing law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio without regard to conflicts of law principles.
Execution: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.
