VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Agreement is entered into by and between Northbridge Health Plan ("Health Plan") and Aurelia Health Services, LLC ("Vendor").
The Effective Date of this Agreement is July 24, 2026.
The Initial Term of the Agreement shall end on July 23, 2028.
Vendor shall provide professional services to the Health Plan as described in Exhibit A and in accordance with applicable law and industry standards.
Lines of business covered by this Agreement include Medicare Advantage, Medicaid.
Fees, rates and payment procedures are set forth in Exhibit B and invoices shall be paid Net 30 from the invoice date.
All charges must be supported by reasonable documentation and conform to the Payment terms and fee schedules agreed herein.
This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of nonrenewal at least 120 days prior to the then-current term end.
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon providing 120 days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure the breach within 30 days of written notice of breach.
Vendor shall maintain patient confidentiality and comply with all applicable privacy and security laws, including but not limited to HIPAA.
Each party agrees to indemnify, defend and hold harmless the other party, its affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from claims arising out of their respective negligent acts or willful misconduct.
Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber liability and workers' compensation insurance in amounts customary for the services performed and naming the Health Plan as an additional insured where applicable.
Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's books, records and operations related to the services, upon reasonable prior written notice, to verify compliance and billing accuracy.
Vendor shall cooperate with audits and provide access to records, facilities and personnel during normal business hours; audit findings giving rise to overpayments shall be promptly remitted to Health Plan.
Any subcontracts that materially affect performance under this Agreement require Health Plan's prior written consent and Vendor remains liable for subcontractor performance.
Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, or overnight courier to the addresses set forth in Exhibit C and shall be effective upon receipt.
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that either party may assign to an affiliate or successor in interest.
Each party represents and warrants that it has all necessary corporate power and authority to enter into and perform its obligations under this Agreement.
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
If any provision of this Agreement is found invalid or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement shall continue in full force and effect.
The remedies provided herein are cumulative and not exclusive of any remedies provided by law.
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California without regard to conflict of laws principles.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have caused this Agreement to be executed by their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.
