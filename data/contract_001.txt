Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective January 9, 2026, by and between Hudson Valley Care Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and LoneStar Care Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Term: The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues through January 8, 2029, unless earlier terminated in accordance with this Agreement.
Services: Vendor shall provide services to the Health Plan in the lines of business identified as Medicaid, as set forth in applicable statements of work.
Payment terms: Net 45. Health Plan shall pay Vendor for Services in accordance with agreed rates and invoices submitted by Vendor, with invoices paid within Net 45 days from receipt of a properly documented invoice.
Auto-Renewal: Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days prior to the then-current term expiration, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms under the same terms and conditions.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 60 days' written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach remains uncured thirty days after written notice specifying the breach.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's confidential information and shall use such information only to perform its obligations under this Agreement.
Protected Health Information: Vendor agrees to comply with all applicable federal and state laws governing protected health information and shall enter into a business associate agreement if required.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan from and against claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses arising out of Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct; Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's negligence.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber/privacy liability, and workers' compensation insurance with limits reasonably acceptable to Health Plan and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit Rights: Health Plan or its designee shall have the right to audit Vendor's records and facilities related to the provision of Services upon reasonable notice during normal business hours and in a manner designed to minimize disruption.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable laws, regulations and Health Plan policies relevant to the performance of Services, including applicable Medicaid rules.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent, and shall remain responsible for the acts and omissions of any approved subcontractors.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain appropriate administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect Health Plan data and shall promptly notify Health Plan of any security incident.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality, and willful misconduct, neither party's liability shall exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the twelve months preceding the claim.
Notices: All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, or nationally recognized overnight courier to the parties' addresses specified in this Agreement or as later designated in writing.
Records Retention: Vendor shall retain records necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement and applicable laws for at least seven years or longer if required by law.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve disputes amicably before pursuing formal remedies.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to conflicts of law principles.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that Health Plan may assign to an affiliate or successor.
Severability: If any provision of this Agreement is held invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with any exhibits and statements of work, constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
Signatures: IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
