VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT between Hudson Valley Care Plan, Inc. (the "Health Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC (the "Vendor").
This Agreement is effective as of February 5, 2025.
Vendor will provide clinical and administrative services described in Exhibit A for the Health Plan's Commercial line of business.
The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and end on February 4, 2028.
Unless either party gives written notice of nonrenewal at least 90 days prior to the then-current term expiration, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
Compensation, fee schedules and billing procedures are set forth in Exhibit B; all payments due under this Agreement shall be paid Net 45 days from receipt of undisputed invoice (Net 45).
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice of breach.
Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws, rules, regulations and payer requirements, including privacy and security obligations applicable to protected health information.
The Vendor agrees to maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber liability, and workers' compensation insurance in amounts customary for the services provided and as specified in Exhibit C.
Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Health Plan and its officers, directors and employees from third-party claims arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement.
The Health Plan shall indemnify and hold Vendor harmless for claims arising from the Health Plan's negligence or willful misconduct to the extent permitted by law.
Each party's indemnification obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement.
Vendor shall maintain confidentiality of Health Plan information and member data and shall use such information only to perform its obligations hereunder; confidentiality obligations shall survive termination.
Vendor shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect member information and shall report any unauthorized disclosures in accordance with applicable law.
The Health Plan reserves the right to audit Vendor's records, systems and processes related to services provided, upon reasonable notice and during regular business hours, for up to three years after termination.
Vendor will cooperate with audits and provide requested documentation and access, and will reimburse the Health Plan for any overpayments identified as a result of such audits in accordance with Exhibit D.
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except that the Health Plan may assign to an affiliate or successor by operation of law.
Notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the contact information set forth on the signature page, and shall be effective upon receipt.
Except where preempted by federal law, this Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida.
The parties agree to seek resolution of disputes promptly and in good faith, and may pursue injunctive relief where necessary to protect confidential or proprietary information.
Limitation of liability shall be as set forth in Exhibit E, except for liabilities arising from breach of confidentiality, indemnification obligations, or willful misconduct.
The representations, warranties and covenants of the parties are set forth herein, and remedies provided are cumulative and in addition to any rights at law or equity.
This Agreement, together with Exhibits A through E and any amendments signed by both parties, constitutes the entire agreement between Hudson Valley Care Plan, Inc. and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement through their authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.
