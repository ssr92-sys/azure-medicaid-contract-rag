Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") between BayPoint Health Plan, Inc. ("Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC ("Vendor").
This Agreement is effective as of April 8, 2026 and sets forth terms for services to be provided by Vendor.
The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and ends on April 7, 2027.
This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Scope: Vendor will provide clinical and related services to members of the Plan in the following lines of business: Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, Commercial.
Services shall be performed in accordance with applicable law, Plan policies, and standards described in any attached Statement(s) of Work.
Payment: Plan will pay Vendor according to the rates and fee schedule agreed by the parties; all invoices are due Net 60 from receipt of a proper invoice.
Vendor shall submit invoices in the form and detail reasonably required by the Plan.
Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate for cause if the other party materially breaches and fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice of breach.
Confidentiality: Each party shall protect Confidential Information of the other and only use it as necessary to perform under this Agreement.
Vendor agrees to comply with applicable privacy and security laws, including HIPAA, and to implement appropriate safeguards for member data.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Plan and its officers, directors and employees from claims arising out of Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
The Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from the Plan's gross negligence or willful misconduct to the extent permitted by law.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain general liability, professional liability and cyber liability insurance in amounts customary for the industry and shall provide certificates upon request.
Audit Rights: Plan may audit Vendor's records, billing and performance related to this Agreement during normal business hours upon reasonable notice.
Record Retention: Vendor shall retain records supporting services and invoices for at least seven (7) years or as otherwise required by law.
Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, overnight courier, or certified mail to the addresses of the parties set forth in the preamble or as otherwise designated.
Compliance: Vendor warrants that it will comply with all applicable federal and state laws, regulations and regulatory program requirements relevant to the services.
Independent Contractor; neither party is an agent, partner or joint venturer of the other and Vendor is responsible for its employees and subcontractors.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification, willful misconduct, fraud, or violation of law, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid under this Agreement in the prior twelve (12) months.
Assignment is prohibited without the other party's prior written consent, except to a successor in interest by merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Dispute Resolution: Parties will attempt to resolve disputes through good faith negotiations before pursuing judicial remedies.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California.
Entire Agreement: This document, together with any exhibits or Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement and may be executed in counterparts and electronically.
