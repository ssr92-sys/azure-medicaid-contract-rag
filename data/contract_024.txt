VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between GulfHealth Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Suncoast Care Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Effective date: January 10, 2026.
Scope: Vendor will provide services as set forth in mutually agreed Statements of Work covering the Health Plan's lines of business: Commercial.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement commences on the Effective Date and ends on January 9, 2027.
This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Payment: Fees shall be invoiced in accordance with the applicable Statement of Work and payments shall be due Net 45 from receipt of a correct invoice.
Termination for convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement or any Statement of Work for convenience upon 30 days written notice to the other party.
Termination for cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice of such breach.
Confidentiality: Each party shall protect the other party's Confidential Information and shall not disclose or use such information except as necessary to perform under this Agreement.
Data protection: Vendor shall implement administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect member data and shall comply with applicable privacy laws, including where applicable HIPAA.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan and its officers, directors and employees from and against claims, losses and liabilities arising from Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance, including professional liability and general liability coverage, and shall provide certificates upon request.
Audit rights: Health Plan or its designees shall have the right, upon reasonable notice, to audit Vendor's records related to services, payments and compliance during normal business hours.
Records retention: Vendor shall retain and make available records related to services and billing for a minimum of seven years after termination or longer if required by law.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered by certified mail, overnight courier, or electronic delivery with confirmation to the addresses provided in the applicable Statement of Work.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except to a successor in interest by merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Limitation of liability: Except for indemnification obligations and willful misconduct, neither party shall be liable for consequential, incidental or punitive damages.
Compliance: Vendor represents that it will comply with all applicable federal and state laws, regulations and Health Plan policies in performing services.
Independent contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner or agent of Health Plan; Vendor is solely responsible for its employees and subcontractors.
Severability: If any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall remain in full force and effect without the invalid provision.
Governing law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, without regard to conflict of law principles.
Amendment: This Agreement may be amended only by a written instrument signed by authorized representatives of both parties.
Entire agreement: This Agreement, including all Statements of Work and exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date by their duly authorized representatives.
