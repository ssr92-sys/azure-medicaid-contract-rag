VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement is entered into by and between BayPoint Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC ("Vendor").
Effective date: February 13, 2025.
Scope of Services: Vendor will provide clinical and administrative services as set forth in the attached Statement of Work covering the Health Plan's Medicare Advantage and Medicaid lines of business.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue through February 12, 2027.
Auto-Renewal: Unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 120 days prior to the then-current term end, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon providing not less than 120 days' prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Payment Terms: Fees shall be invoiced monthly and paid by Health Plan within sixty (60) days of receipt of a correct invoice (Net 60).
Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right, upon reasonable notice during normal business hours, to audit Vendor's books, records and systems relating to services and charges.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's Protected Health Information and other confidential information in accordance with applicable law and this Agreement.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations, including those applicable to Medicare Advantage and Medicaid.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan and its affiliates from and against claims, losses, liabilities and expenses arising from Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain insurance coverages customary for its industry, including professional liability, general liability and cyber insurance, and provide certificates upon request.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect member data and report any security incidents promptly.
Subcontracting: Vendor shall not delegate or subcontract material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent, and shall remain responsible for subcontractor performance.
Performance Standards: Vendor shall meet performance metrics and service levels specified in the Statement of Work, and report regularly on key performance indicators.
Remedies: In addition to termination and indemnification, the parties' remedies shall include specific performance, setoff and any other remedies available at law or equity.
Records Retention: Vendor shall retain records related to services for a minimum of seven years or as required by law, whichever is longer.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at the addresses set forth herein or subsequently provided, and shall be effective upon receipt.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to a successor in interest in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and not an employee, joint venturer or agent of Health Plan.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith, and any unresolved disputes shall be subject to binding arbitration in the State of Ohio if agreed or litigated in state court as required.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with the Statement of Work and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes prior understandings.
