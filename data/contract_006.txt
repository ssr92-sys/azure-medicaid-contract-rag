VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Clearwater Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Aurelia Health Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Effective Date: August 29, 2025.
The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall end on August 28, 2026.
Lines of business covered: Medicaid.
This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Services: Vendor shall provide services as set forth in attached statements of work, including but not limited to care coordination, claims support, and provider outreach for Medicaid members.
Payment Terms: Health Plan shall pay Vendor in accordance with the agreed fee schedule, with all undisputed invoices payable Net 60 from the invoice date.
Taxes and withholdings shall be the responsibility of the party required by law to pay them unless otherwise agreed in writing.
Termination: Either party may terminate for convenience upon 120 days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information and protected health information in compliance with state and federal law.
Vendor shall implement appropriate safeguards to protect PHI and shall execute a Business Associate Agreement if required.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan and its officers, directors and employees from third-party claims arising from Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising solely from Health Plan's negligence in performing its obligations.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber liability and workers' compensation insurance in amounts reasonably acceptable to Health Plan and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's records and invoices related to this Agreement upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours, subject to confidentiality protections.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws, regulations and Health Plan policies applicable to Medicaid services.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect electronic protected health information and notify Health Plan of any security incidents in accordance with applicable law.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations, breach of confidentiality or willful misconduct, the parties' aggregate liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.
Notices: All notices required hereunder shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth herein or to such other address as a party designates by written notice; notices by overnight courier or certified mail shall be deemed received on the date of delivery.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party, except that Health Plan may assign to an affiliate or successor.
Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or joint venture relationship.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio without regard to conflict of law principles.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with any exhibits and statements of work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.
