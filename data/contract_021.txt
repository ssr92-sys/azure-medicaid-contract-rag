VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between GulfHealth Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and LoneStar Care Services, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of July 15, 2025.
1. Term: The Initial Term of this Agreement commences on July 15, 2025 and shall continue through July 14, 2028, unless earlier terminated as provided herein.
2. Scope: Vendor shall provide services to the Health Plan for the Lines of Business covered: Commercial, as described in Exhibit A.
3. Payment: Health Plan shall pay Vendor in accordance with the Payment terms: Net 30 from receipt of a proper invoice.
4. Auto-Renewal: Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party delivers written notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days prior to the end of the then-current term.
5. Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
6. Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate for material breach if such breach remains uncured thirty (30) days after written notice describing the breach.
7. Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary and patient information and shall comply with all applicable privacy and security laws and regulations.
8. Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan, its officers and directors from and against any third-party claims arising out of Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct; Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's gross negligence.
9. Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercially reasonable insurance including professional liability, general liability and cyber liability insurance, with limits reasonably acceptable to Health Plan and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
10. Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's books, records and systems relevant to performance and billing upon reasonable advance notice and during regular business hours.
11. Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all federal and state laws and regulations applicable to the performance of services under this Agreement.
12. Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect data and shall notify Health Plan without unreasonable delay upon discovery of any security incident.
13. Fees and Expenses: Except as otherwise agreed in writing, each party shall bear its own costs and expenses in performing under this Agreement.
14. Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations, negligence, willful misconduct, and breaches of confidentiality, neither party shall be liable to the other for consequential, incidental or punitive damages.
15. Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, which shall not be unreasonably withheld, except to a successor in interest.
16. Notices: All notices required hereunder shall be in writing and delivered by hand, certified mail, or overnight courier to the addresses set forth below or to such other address as a party may designate in writing.
17. Remedies and Severability: The rights and remedies provided in this Agreement are cumulative; if any provision is held invalid, the remainder shall continue in full force and effect.
18. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with all exhibits and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements relating to the subject matter hereof.
19. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio, without regard to its conflicts of law principles.
20. Counterparts: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original and all of which together shall constitute one instrument.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date set forth above.
GulfHealth Plan, Inc.                 LoneStar Care Services, LLC
