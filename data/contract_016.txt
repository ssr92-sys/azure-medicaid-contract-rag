Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into effective April 7, 2025 between BayPoint Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and LoneStar Care Services, LLC ("Vendor").
1. Scope of Services: Vendor will provide services to the Health Plan for the lines of business covered: Medicare Advantage and Medicaid, as further described in attached exhibits.
2. Term: The initial term of this Agreement commences on April 7, 2025 and ends on April 6, 2028.
3. Auto-Renewal: Unless terminated as provided herein, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 90 days prior to the then-current term end.
4. Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon 90 days' prior written notice to the other party.
5. Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty days after written notice.
6. Payment Terms: Health Plan will pay Vendor in accordance with agreed rates; all undisputed invoices shall be paid Net 45.
7. Taxes and Withholdings: Each party is responsible for its own taxes, withholdings and reporting as required by applicable law.
8. Confidentiality: Each party shall protect confidential information of the other with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information and shall not disclose such information except as permitted by this Agreement or required by law.
9. Data Security and PHI: Vendor will maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect Protected Health Information in compliance with applicable law and Health Plan policies.
10. Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify and hold harmless Health Plan and its affiliates, officers and employees from third-party claims arising from Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct; Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's negligence.
11. Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability and cyber liability insurance in amounts reasonable for the services provided and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
12. Audit Rights: Health Plan or its designee may audit Vendor's books, records and facilities related to performance, billing and compliance upon reasonable notice during normal business hours.
13. Subcontracting: Vendor may subcontract performance only with Health Plan's prior written consent and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any subcontractors.
14. Compliance with Law: Vendor and Health Plan shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations, including those governing Medicare Advantage and Medicaid.
15. Limitation of Liability: Except for willful misconduct or indemnification obligations, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed amounts paid under this Agreement in the twelve months preceding the claim.
16. Remedies: The rights and remedies set forth in this Agreement are cumulative and not exclusive of any rights available at law or in equity.
17. Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at their respective addresses in the signature block, by certified mail, overnight courier, or email with confirmation.
18. Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or successor in interest.
19. Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship.
20. Severability and Waiver: If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder shall remain in effect; waiver of any breach shall not constitute waiver of subsequent breaches.
21. Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with exhibits and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements relating to its subject matter.
22. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas without regard to conflict of law principles.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the effective date above.
