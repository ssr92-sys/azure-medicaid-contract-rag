VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of June 9, 2025, by and between Clearwater Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and LoneStar Care Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue through June 8, 2026.
Renewal: The parties expressly agree that this Agreement does NOT automatically renew and must be the subject of a written extension to continue beyond the initial term.
Services: Vendor shall provide services to the Health Plan in connection with the Health Plan's lines of business, specifically Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and Commercial.
Scope: The specific scope of services, service levels, and performance metrics shall be set forth in Exhibit A attached hereto and incorporated by reference.
Compensation: In consideration for the Services, the Health Plan shall pay Vendor according to the fees and fee schedule set forth in Exhibit B.
Payment Terms: All undisputed invoices are payable Net 60 from receipt of a proper invoice unless otherwise agreed in writing.
Taxes and Withholding: Each party shall be responsible for its own taxes and withholding obligations, and Vendor shall provide tax documentation upon request.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon sixty (60) days prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately for material breach if such breach is not cured within thirty (30) days after written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other party's Confidential Information and to use such information only for performance under this Agreement.
Data Protection: Vendor shall comply with applicable privacy and security laws and the Health Plan's data protection policies when handling PHI or other sensitive information.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Health Plan and its affiliates from claims arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
Limitation of Liability: Except for payment obligations, indemnification, and willful misconduct, neither party's aggregate liability shall exceed the fees paid by the Health Plan to Vendor in the prior twelve (12) months.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber liability, and workers' compensation insurance in amounts reasonably sufficient for the Services and provide certificates upon request.
Audit Rights: The Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's relevant records and systems during normal business hours with reasonable advance notice to verify compliance and billing accuracy.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material obligations without the Health Plan's prior written consent, and Vendor shall remain responsible for subcontractor performance.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws, including those governing Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and commercial plan operations.
Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth below, by overnight courier, certified mail, or email with confirmation of receipt.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith, and, if unresolved, any legal action shall be brought in state or federal courts located in the State of New York.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflicts of law principles.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with its Exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions and agreements.
Execution: The parties have executed this Agreement as of the dates set forth below. Clearwater Health Plan, Inc. / LoneStar Care Services, LLC
