VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of August 1, 2025 by and between BayPoint Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Medora Solutions, LLC ("Vendor").
Recitals: Health Plan desires to procure certain administrative and clinical support services for its Medicare Advantage line of business, and Vendor represents it has expertise to provide such services.
1. Services: Vendor will provide the services described in the attached Statement of Work, including but not limited to claims processing support, care coordination, and reporting for Medicare Advantage members.
2. Term: The Effective Date is August 1, 2025 and the Initial Term shall end on July 31, 2026.
This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
3. Payment: Health Plan shall pay Vendor in accordance with the fee schedule; all invoices are due Net 30 from receipt unless otherwise mutually agreed in writing.
4. Termination: Either party may terminate for convenience upon ninety (90) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within thirty (30) days after written notice.
5. Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain as confidential all proprietary, member, and plan information received from the other and shall use such information solely for performance under this Agreement.
6. Data Security and HIPAA: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect PHI and comply with applicable federal and state privacy laws.
7. Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Health Plan and its officers from claims arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or failure to comply with applicable laws.
Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's gross negligence or intentional misconduct.
8. Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability/errors and omissions, and cyber liability insurance with limits reasonably acceptable to Health Plan and shall provide certificates upon request.
9. Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right, during normal business hours and upon reasonable notice, to audit Vendor's records and systems pertinent to the services and charging practices.
10. Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all federal and state laws applicable to Medicare Advantage, CMS requirements, and Health Plan policies.
11. Notices: All notices under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at their respective addresses set forth below or as later designated in writing.
Notices shall be effective upon delivery by hand, overnight courier, or three (3) days after deposit in the U.S. mail, postage prepaid.
12. Assignment and Subcontracting: Vendor may not assign or subcontract material obligations without Health Plan's prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld.
13. Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement creates an employer-employee, partnership, or joint venture relationship.
14. Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations and willful misconduct, neither party's liability shall exceed the fees paid by Health Plan to Vendor in the twelve months preceding the claim.
15. Survival: Provisions concerning confidentiality, indemnification, insurance, audit rights and limitation of liability shall survive expiration or termination of this Agreement.
16. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas, without regard to conflict of law principles.
17. Entire Agreement: This document, together with any exhibits or Statements of Work, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior discussions.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement through their duly authorized representatives as of the Effective Date.
