Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into between GulfHealth Plan, Inc. ("Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC ("Vendor") effective as of March 12, 2025.
Services: Vendor shall provide such clinical and related services as set forth in applicable Statements of Work to support the Plan's lines of business covered, specifically Medicaid.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue until March 11, 2028 (the "Initial Term").
Renewal: This Agreement does NOT automatically renew; any extension or renewal must be by written amendment executed by both parties.
Termination: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Material breach permitting termination for cause shall be subject to a thirty (30) day cure period unless immediate termination is permitted by law.
Compensation: The Plan shall pay Vendor for services in accordance with the rates set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.
Payment Terms: Unless otherwise set forth in a Statement of Work, all undisputed invoices shall be paid Net 45 from the Plan's receipt of a proper invoice.
Taxes and Withholding: Each party shall be responsible for its own taxes and withholdings as required by applicable law.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of the other's proprietary information and protected health information in accordance with applicable law, including HIPAA, and shall not disclose such information except as required or permitted under this Agreement.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Plan and its officers, directors and employees from and against any third-party claims, losses or damages arising from Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement; the Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from the Plan's negligence or breach.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, and cyber/privacy insurance in amounts reasonably acceptable to the Plan and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit Rights: The Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's records, books and billing related to services provided under this Agreement, upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours, to verify compliance and proper invoicing.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material obligations hereunder without the Plan's prior written consent, and Vendor shall remain liable for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations applicable to the performance of services under this Agreement, including those specific to Medicaid.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain reasonable administrative, physical and technical safeguards to protect Plan data and member information.
Remedies: Except as otherwise provided, remedies are cumulative and in addition to any other rights or remedies provided by law.
Limitation of Liability: Except for damages arising from a party's gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality or indemnification obligations, liability shall be limited to direct damages not to exceed amounts paid under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
Notices: All notices required or permitted hereunder shall be in writing and delivered to the parties at their respective addresses set forth in the introductory paragraph, or as otherwise notified in writing, and shall be effective upon receipt.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's prior written consent, provided that the Plan may assign to an affiliate or successor entity.
Records Retention: Vendor shall retain all records related to Medicaid services for the period required by applicable law and shall make such records available to the Plan and governmental authorities upon request.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute arising under this Agreement; if unresolved, the dispute shall be resolved in the courts of the State of California.
Independent Contractor: Vendor is an independent contractor and nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employer-employee or joint venture relationship.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with any attached Statements of Work and exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements and understandings.
