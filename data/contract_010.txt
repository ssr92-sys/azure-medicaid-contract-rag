Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Hudson Valley Care Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Medora Solutions, LLC ("Vendor") effective March 24, 2026.
Recitals: Health Plan desires services for its lines of business specified below and Vendor has capacity to provide them.
Lines of business covered: Medicare Advantage, Medicaid.
Services: Vendor shall perform services described in attached exhibits and any mutually executed statements of work.
Term: The initial term of this Agreement shall commence on March 24, 2026 and shall end on March 23, 2029.
This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Payment Terms: Invoices submitted by Vendor shall be payable Net 45 unless otherwise agreed in writing.
Fees, billing procedures and withholding responsibilities shall be as set forth in exhibits.
Termination: Either party may terminate for convenience upon 120 days' prior written notice.
Either party may terminate for material breach if the breaching party fails to cure within a specified cure period.
Confidentiality: Each party shall protect Confidential Information of the other using at least the same care it uses for its own confidential materials.
Confidential Information excludes information that is public, independently developed or rightfully received from third parties.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan and its affiliates from third party claims arising from Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Health Plan shall indemnify Vendor for claims arising from Health Plan's negligence, breach of this Agreement or misuse of services.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability and cyber insurance in amounts reasonably sufficient and as required in the exhibits.
Audit Rights: Health Plan or its designated auditors may audit Vendor's compliance with this Agreement and programmatic and financial records upon reasonable notice and during normal business hours.
Records supporting invoices shall be retained by Vendor for a minimum of six years for inspection.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in the exhibits, by certified mail, courier or email with confirmation.
Data Security and HIPAA: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical and technical safeguards required by applicable law to protect PHI and personal data.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and program requirements applicable to Medicare Advantage and Medicaid.
Limitation of Liability: Except for indemnification obligations and willful misconduct, liability of each party shall be limited to direct damages not to exceed amounts paid under the Agreement in the prior twelve months.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to a successor in interest.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California.
Signatures: The parties by their authorized representatives have executed this Agreement below as of the Effective Date.
