VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of December 11, 2025 by and between Northbridge Health Plan ("Health Plan") and Aurelia Health Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Services: Vendor shall provide services to the Health Plan in connection with the Medicare Advantage line of business as described in Exhibit A.
Term: The initial term shall commence on December 11, 2025 and shall end on December 10, 2026.
Renewal: This Agreement does NOT automatically renew.
Fees and Payment: Health Plan shall pay Vendor as set forth in Exhibit B; all undisputed invoices are payable Net 45 days from receipt.
Taxes: Each party is responsible for its own taxes unless otherwise required by law.
Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate for material breach that remains uncured after thirty (30) days written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary and protected health information received under this Agreement in accordance with applicable law.
Data Security: Vendor shall implement and maintain administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect member data and shall notify Health Plan of any breach promptly.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless Health Plan, its affiliates and their officers, directors and employees from third party claims arising out of Vendor's negligence or willful misconduct.
Mutual Indemnity: Each party's indemnification obligations shall be subject to limits and procedures set forth in Exhibit C.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain comprehensive general liability, professional liability, and cyber insurance in amounts customary for the services and as specified in Exhibit D.
Audit Rights: Health Plan shall have the right, upon reasonable notice, to audit Vendor's records and systems relating to performance, billing and compliance for a period of three years.
Subcontracting: Vendor may not subcontract material services without Health Plan's prior written approval and shall remain responsible for subcontractor performance.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws, including HIPAA, Medicare Advantage program rules, and State of Florida statutes applicable to the services.
Limitation of Liability: Except for willful misconduct, gross negligence, or indemnified third-party claims, liability of each party shall be limited as set forth in Exhibit E.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses specified herein or as updated by written notice; notices are effective upon receipt.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to a successor in interest in a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Dispute Resolution: The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith and may pursue litigation if resolution is not achieved, subject to the governing law clause.
Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida without regard to conflict of law principles.
Independent Contractors: The parties are independent contractors; nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or joint venture relationship.
Entire Agreement: This Agreement, together with its exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement between Northbridge Health Plan and Aurelia Health Services, LLC and supersedes prior understandings.
Execution: This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and by electronic signature; signed by authorized representatives of Northbridge Health Plan and Aurelia Health Services, LLC.
