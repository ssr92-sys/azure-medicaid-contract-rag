Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of October 8, 2025 by and between Evergreen Health Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Aurelia Health Services, LLC ("Vendor").
Engagement: Vendor will provide services described in Exhibit A to support the Health Plan's operations and members.
Lines of business covered: Medicare Advantage and Medicaid.
Term: The Initial Term commences on the Effective Date and ends on October 7, 2026.
Auto-Renewal: Unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive one-year terms.
Termination for convenience: Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon providing at least one hundred twenty (120) days' prior written notice to the other party.
Payment terms: Fees shall be invoiced in accordance with Exhibit B and paid by the Health Plan Net 30 from receipt of a proper invoice.
Setoff and adjustments: Health Plan may withhold or set off payments to the extent permitted by law and for amounts disputed in good faith.
Confidentiality: Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of non-public information received from the other and use such information only to perform obligations under this Agreement.
Data security and privacy: Vendor shall maintain administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the services, comply with HIPAA and applicable state privacy laws, and report breaches in accordance with applicable timelines.
Indemnification: Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Health Plan and its officers, directors and affiliates from third-party claims arising out of Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
Limitation of liability: Except for indemnification obligations, gross negligence, willful misconduct, and breaches of confidentiality or data security, each party's aggregate liability shall be limited to direct damages not to exceed fees paid under this Agreement in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability/errors & omissions, and cyber liability insurance in commercially reasonable amounts and provide certificates upon request.
Audit rights: Health Plan retains the right to audit Vendor's books, records and system logs related to services and billed fees upon reasonable notice during normal business hours.
Records retention: Vendor shall retain service records and supporting documentation for at least seven years or as otherwise required by law.
Subcontracting: Vendor may subcontract performance only with prior written consent of the Health Plan and remains responsible for the acts and omissions of any subcontractor.
Performance standards: Vendor shall provide services consistent with industry standards and the service levels set forth in Exhibit B and shall remedy deficiencies promptly.
Compliance with laws: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations governing Medicare Advantage and Medicaid programs and related requirements.
Notices: All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth in Exhibit C, and shall be effective upon delivery or three days after deposit in the United States mail, postage prepaid.
Dispute resolution: The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve disputes promptly and may pursue mediation prior to initiating litigation.
Assignment: Neither party may assign its rights or obligations without the other party's prior written consent, except to an affiliate or in connection with a merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Survival: Provisions regarding confidentiality, indemnification, payment, insurance, audit rights and governing law shall survive expiration or termination.
Governing law: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Florida without regard to conflicts of law principles.
Execution: The parties, through their duly authorized representatives, have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
