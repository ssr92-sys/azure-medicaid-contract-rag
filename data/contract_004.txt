Vendor Services Agreement
This Vendor Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between GulfHealth Plan, Inc. ("Health Plan") and Bayline Clinical Partners, LLC ("Vendor") effective August 21, 2026.
Purpose: Vendor will provide clinical and care coordination services as set forth in applicable scopes of work for the Health Plan.
Lines of business covered: Medicare Advantage, Medicaid.
Term: The Effective date of this Agreement is August 21, 2026 and the Initial term ends: August 20, 2027.
THIS AGREEMENT DOES NOT AUTOMATICALLY RENEW.
Payment terms: Net 45; Vendor shall invoice the Health Plan and payment will be made within forty-five (45) days of receipt of a proper invoice.
Termination for convenience notice: 30 days; either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience upon thirty (30) days prior written notice.
Confidentiality: Each party will maintain in confidence all Protected Health Information and proprietary information received from the other and will use such information only to perform under this Agreement.
Indemnification: Vendor agrees to indemnify, defend and hold harmless the Health Plan from third party claims arising from Vendor's negligence, willful misconduct or breach of this Agreement; the Health Plan will indemnify Vendor for claims arising from the Health Plan's material breach.
Insurance: Vendor shall maintain commercial general liability, professional liability, cyber liability and workers' compensation insurance in commercially reasonable amounts and shall provide certificates of insurance upon request.
Audit rights: The Health Plan shall have the right to audit Vendor's books, records and systems relevant to performance and billing, with reasonable prior notice and during normal business hours.
Compliance: Vendor shall comply with all applicable federal and state laws and regulations, including but not limited to HIPAA, Medicare and Medicaid program requirements.
Data security and breach notification: Vendor shall implement reasonable safeguards and shall notify the Health Plan without undue delay upon discovery of any security incident involving Plan data.
Subcontracting: Vendor may subcontract services only with the prior written consent of the Health Plan and remains responsible for subcontractor performance.
Relationship of the parties: The parties are independent contractors; nothing herein creates a joint venture, partnership or employment relationship.
Limitation of liability: Except for indemnification obligations and willful misconduct, neither party's liability shall exceed the aggregate fees paid under this Agreement during the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
Notices: All notices required under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses of the parties by certified mail, overnight courier, or email with confirmation.
Assignment: Neither party may assign this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other, except to a successor in interest by merger or sale of substantially all assets.
Governing law: State of California governs the interpretation and enforcement of this Agreement.
Audit retention and records: Vendor shall retain relevant records for six (6) years and shall cooperate with any government or payer audits.
Severability and waiver: If any provision is found unenforceable, remaining provisions shall survive; waiver of any breach shall not constitute a waiver of subsequent breaches.
Survival: Sections concerning confidentiality, indemnification, audit rights, insurance and governing law shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.
Entire agreement: This document, including attached scopes of work and exhibits, constitutes the entire agreement and may be amended only by a written instrument signed by both parties.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective date written above by their duly authorized representatives.
